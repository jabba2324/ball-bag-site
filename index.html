Ball Bag Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Ball Bag is a casual ball-merging game for iOS and Android. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short, Ball Bag does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Ball Bag has no accounts or sign-in and no analytics, tracking or advertising. It does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and it makes no network requests.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your device so it can show them the next time you play:

  • Your high scores, with the date of each score and the largest ball reached
  • Your best score
  • Your settings (night mode and haptics on or off)

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Sharing your score

If you tap a share button, Ball Bag opens your device's standard share sheet with a short text message about your score. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app or contact to share with. Anything you share is then handled by the app you chose, under that app's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Ball Bag collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
